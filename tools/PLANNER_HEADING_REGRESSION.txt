Planner heading regression (2026-10-21)

Diagnosis:
  In the original 100 Full_Warehouse episodes, iPlanner had 40 and ViPlanner
  had 39 non-collision episodes ending at exactly 4.5 simulation seconds
  with effectively zero displacement. Every such case had an initial goal
  more than 90 degrees behind the robot. There were 42 behind-goal episodes.
  The ordinary MPC has nonnegative speed, zero heading tracking weight and
  a 1.5-second horizon. Behind-path inputs reproduce near-zero speed AND yaw
  commands with its zero initialization. This is not an Isaac process hang.

Correction:
  --controller lookahead explicitly selects ModeMPCController for ALL
  policies. This is the existing controller used by GRPO-rule, including
  its far-position and far-heading objective; model weights, inference,
  generated waypoints, speed limits and termination rules are unchanged.
  --controller native preserves historical routing for reproducibility.
  No switch based on model identity, reward or success is introduced.
  Both smoke and continuous-suite configs support controller=lookahead.

Tests:
  OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=<repo> \
    <Isaac Python> <repo>/tests/test_heading_mpc.py
  Covers native backward deadlock, left/right/180-degree turning, forward
  tracking and invariance to world translation/rotation. CPU only.
  Live smoke runs episodes 7,8,9 in ONE process per model, preserving reset
  and continuous-rollout behavior. control_debug.jsonl records commanded
  and measured velocities, robot pose, goal and selected world trajectory.

Reporting:
  Improved ability to start moving is not proof of task success or collision
  avoidance. Compare complete metrics and videos. Do not merge repaired
  controller results with historical native-controller rows. Full reruns
  require a new output directory and an explicit lookahead configuration.
